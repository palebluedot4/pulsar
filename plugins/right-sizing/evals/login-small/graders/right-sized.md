PASS if the decision is proportionate to one part-time developer, 2,000
members and about 20 USD a month: a managed identity service, or sign-in
built into the app with server-side sessions and a maintained OAuth/OIDC
client library for Google (building sign-in into the app this way is not
running an identity provider). It must also reject at least one alternative
with a reason tied to the stated scale, team or budget.

FAIL if it recommends running, building or self-hosting an identity provider
(such as an OpenID Provider or Keycloak) or adopting bank-grade profiles such
as FAPI, or if it rejects alternatives without any reason tied to the stated
context.
